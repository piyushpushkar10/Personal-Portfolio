Engineer by training, problem-solver by habit

I'm an Application Developer at Invenia (demerged from Sterlite Technologies Limited, Vedanta Group) in Gurugram, with 1.5+ years of building software that real operations teams rely on every day.

My work sits where engineering meets the business.
